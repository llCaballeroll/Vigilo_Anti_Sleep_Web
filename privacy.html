Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

In short

  • Vigilo does not collect any data. We have no servers, accounts, analytics, ads or trackers, and the app has no network code of its own.
  • To recognize AI agent sessions, Vigilo reads the names and paths of the processes running on your Mac. This happens only on your Mac and is never stored or sent anywhere.
  • Your settings are saved only on your Mac.

How AI Mode works

While AI Mode is on, Vigilo checks the list of processes that belong to your user every few seconds. It looks at the program name, its path, its arguments, its terminal and its working folder to tell whether it is an AI coding agent, and at its CPU time to know whether it is still working. Only the agent name, the folder name and the terminal are shown in the menu. Nothing is written to disk or shared.

Power settings

Vigilo uses public macOS power features to prevent sleep while it is active, and the system setting for sleeping with the lid closed when you turn that option on. It always restores the normal behavior when it turns off.

Data we collect

None. We never receive your processes, projects, settings or any personal information.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, they are created and shown locally by macOS.

Purchases

Vigilo Pro is sold through the Mac App Store. Apple processes the payment and the Apple ID it uses; we never receive your name, email or payment details.

Children

Vigilo is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Since we hold no data about you, there is nothing to access, export or delete on our side. Uninstalling the app removes all its settings from your Mac.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date.
