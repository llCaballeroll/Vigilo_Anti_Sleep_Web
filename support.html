Support

Something not working, or have an idea? Write to soporte.vigilo@outlook.com. Tell us your Mac model, macOS version, your terminal and the agent you use. We usually reply within 2 business days.

Frequently asked questions

I cannot see the cup icon.

The menu bar may be full or hidden behind the notch. Hold Command and drag other icons out of the menu bar, or open Vigilo again from Applications to see its settings.

My agent is not detected.

Vigilo only counts agents running inside a terminal. Check that AI Mode (the sparkle chip) is on and that the agent is enabled in Settings › AI Agents. With Vigilo Pro you can add your own command names.

Which agents does Vigilo recognize?

Claude Code, OpenCode, Codex CLI, Gemini CLI, OpenClaw, GitHub Copilot CLI, Cursor Agent, Aider, Qwen Code, Amp, Goose, Crush, Droid, Kiro, Cline and Kimi, in any terminal: Terminal, iTerm2, Ghostty, Warp, VS Code, Cursor, tmux and more.

Why does my Mac stay awake after I close the agent?

By default Vigilo waits 2 minutes after the last session closes, in case you start another one. With Vigilo Pro you can change or remove that wait.

The lid-closed option does not keep my Mac awake.

Keep your Mac connected to power if possible, and make sure the option is on in Settings › Power (or, with Pro, in Settings › AI Agents). Some macOS updates reset this setting; Vigilo applies it again every 30 seconds.

I bought Vigilo Pro. How do I get it on another Mac?

Open Vigilo Settings › General and click "Restore", signed in with the same Apple ID.
